An XmR chart (Donald Wheeler, Understanding Variation) plots the individual values plus their moving range, with natural process limits derived from the data itself rather than chosen arbitrarily. The centre line is the mean; the upper/lower natural process limits (UNPL/LNPL) are mean ± 2.66·MR̅, where MR̅ is the average moving range between successive points.

A point inside the limits is routine variation (common cause) — leave the process alone. A point or pattern outside is a signal of special cause — investigate. Wheeler's detection rules used here: (1) point outside limits, (2) eight in a row on one side of the centre, (3) three of four beyond the 2/3 line, (4) six consecutive points trending up or down. The MR chart also flags any jump beyond 3.27·MR̅.

Each individual is the median RTT for a 5-minute bin of successful probes for one target. Bins with zero successful probes are omitted (gap, not zero).
